# Idea: "digital prepping"

Preppers are those who prepare for a disaster like catastrophic societal failure.  
They involve various levels of sophistication, from having basic civil defense capabilities to go-bags and secret remote destinations to escape to  
Digital preppers focus on having local IT infrastructure that allows a small group of people to work by themselvs

I think it's a good idea but can be considered a specialist extention to include things like global mesh networks etc.
