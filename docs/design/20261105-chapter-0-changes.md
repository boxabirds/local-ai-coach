## The edits

**1. The shelf** (sets up chapter 1's job table)

Before: "offered her a really short shelf of three or four models, and recommended one."

After: "offered her a really short shelf of three or four models, picked for her box and the job she described, and recommended one."

**2. What the Wednesday window showed** (the check sees the chat app, nothing more; section 12)

Before: "A list, on her screen, that said the only place her client's letter went was a box she owns. No privacy policy involved."

After: "A list, on her screen, that said the only place her chat app sent her client's letter was a box she owns. No privacy policy involved."

**3. A fifth question** (the accountability thread; section 13)

Add after "And why did she check on Wednesday? What could have gone wrong on Tuesday that she wouldn't have seen?":

"And why did she spend twenty minutes on a letter the machine had already written?"

**4. What the book is about** (two silent failures, not one)

Before: "That last one is the question this book is really about, and I'll give you my answer now, because it's the reason I wrote it.

Nearly everything Priya did on Tuesday, a script could do for her.

The Wednesday check is the one thing that has to live in her head, because the failure it guards against is silent."

After: "The last two are the questions this book is really about, and I'll give you my answer now, because it's the reason I wrote it.

Nearly everything Priya did on Tuesday, a script could do for her.

Two things have to live in her head, because the failures they guard against are silent: where her chat app sends things, and what the letter says."

Keep: "Nothing beeps when a draft leaves the building. NOTHING." and the open window. Add one line after the window:

"Nothing beeps when a confident draft gets a date wrong, either."

**5. The chapter list**

Before: "Those four questions take the next five chapters to answer, and we'll take them in order: what's in the box (chapter 1), what's in the file (chapter 2), what a network is and what the internet adds (chapter 3), how she kept the box on her own network and still reached it from home (chapter 4), and how she proved it (chapter 5)."

After: "Those five questions take the next five chapters to answer, and we'll take them in order: what her box can do (chapter 1), what's in the file and why she reads every word it writes (chapter 2), what a network is and what the internet adds (chapter 3), how she kept the box on her own network and still reached it from home (chapter 4), and how she checked her chat app talks to nobody else (chapter 5)."

**6. Stations** ("The map, and three stations")

Priya's three stations keep their ids; their names change where the map shows them: "What can my computer do?" (was "Know your machine") and "Is my chat app talking to anyone else?" (was "Prove it stays local"). "You're accountable" is shown on her path but isn't one of her three this week.
