# Fictional materials for the letter run

Everything here is invented for the book. No real people, properties or cases.

## The request (Priya's words, from chapter 0)

First draft of a letter to the landlord. Firm, not rude. Cite the deposit scheme rules.

## Extract from the tenancy agreement

Assured shorthold tenancy of Flat 2, 14 Saxby Street, Leicester LE2 0ND. Landlord: Mr Graham Pell. Tenant: Ms Aisha Okafor. Term: 1 March 2023 to 28 February 2026, then periodic.

Clause 4.1. The Tenant shall pay a deposit of £1,450. The Landlord shall protect the deposit with the Deposit Protection Service (DPS) and give the Tenant the prescribed information within 30 days of receiving it.

Clause 4.3. At the end of the tenancy the Landlord may deduct from the deposit the reasonable cost of making good any damage caused by the Tenant, fair wear and tear excepted.

Clause 9.2. The Tenant shall leave the Property in the same condition as at the start of the tenancy, as recorded in the check-in inventory dated 1 March 2023, fair wear and tear excepted.

## Facts from the file

- The tenancy ended on 31 August 2026. Ms Okafor returned the keys that day.
- The check-in inventory records the living room walls as "magnolia, freshly painted, good condition".
- The check-out report (3 September 2026) records "light scuffing to living room walls near sofa; two picture hook holes".
- The DPS confirmation shows the deposit was protected on 10 March 2023.
- Mr Pell proposes to keep £900 for "full redecoration of the flat".
- Ms Okafor accepts £60 for filling and touching up the two picture hook holes.
